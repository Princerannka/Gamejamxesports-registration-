2. VALORANT

2.1 Tournament Structure

  • 16 teams
  • 5 main players + 1 substitute
  • Single Elimination
  • Best of 1 (BO1) in every round
  • PC tournament
  • Latest live Valorant patch
  • Entry fee: ₹750 per team

The bracket consists of:

  • Round of 16 — 8 matches
  • Quarterfinals — 4 matches
  • Semifinals — 2 matches
  • Grand Final — 1 match

An optional third-place match may be held between the semifinal losers if time permits.

2.2 Roster

  • 5 main players are required.
  • 1 substitute may be registered.
  • No roster changes after registration closes.
  • Only registered players may participate.

2.3 Seeding

The standard 16-team bracket pairing is:

  • 1 vs 16
  • 8 vs 9
  • 5 vs 12
  • 4 vs 13
  • 3 vs 14
  • 6 vs 11
  • 7 vs 10
  • 2 vs 15

2.4 Map Selection

For BO1:

  1. Knife round or coin toss determines which team bans first.
  2. Teams alternate banning maps.
  3. The remaining map is played.
  4. Server selection is determined by Tournament Officials.

2.5 Game Settings

  • Latest live patch
  • Standard Tournament/Competitive settings
  • All agents are allowed unless disabled by Riot Games
  • Tournament Officials determine server selection

2.6 Match Rules

  • Teams must be ready 15 minutes before their match.
  • Failure to join within 10 minutes may result in a forfeit.
  • Organizers create and manage match lobbies.
  • Cheating, scripting, exploiting and unauthorized software are prohibited.
  • Stream sniping and ghosting are prohibited.
  • Outside assistance is prohibited.
