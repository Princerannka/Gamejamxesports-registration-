GAMEJAM X ESPORTS 2026

OFFICIAL TOURNAMENT RULES

Organizer: SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Ramapuram Venue: MLCP Lab, Ramapuram Campus, Chennai Dates: 28–29 August 2026 Games: Valorant · BGMI · Free Fire · EA SPORTS FC 26 · Tekken 8

1. GENERAL RULES

These rules apply to all five tournaments unless a game-specific rule states otherwise.

1.1 Eligibility

  • The tournament is open to currently enrolled students from any college or university.
  • SRM Ramapuram students and students from other institutions are eligible.
  • Every participant must be a registered and confirmed player.
  • A player may register for only one team/roster across the entire event, regardless of game.
  • Unregistered substitutes are not permitted to participate.
  • Tournament Officials may request a valid college ID at any point before, during, or after a match.
  • Players unable to provide valid identification may be withheld from competition until verification is completed.

1.2 Reporting & Check-In

  • Players/teams must report to their designated venue at least 15 minutes before their scheduled match.
  • Failure to be ready within 10 minutes after the official start time may result in a match forfeit.
  • Players must remain reachable and near their tournament area because schedules may move earlier or later.
  • BGMI teams have a specific requirement to report at least 20 minutes before their first match.

1.3 Equipment

  • Players are responsible for their own equipment where specified.
  • BGMI and Free Fire players must bring their own fully charged mobile devices with the required game version installed.
  • Valorant teams bring their own PCs.
  • EA SPORTS FC 26 and Tekken 8 players must bring their own controllers; the venue provides the projector screen.
  • Players are responsible for their own controller, peripherals and internet stability.
  • Player-side connectivity problems do not automatically qualify for a replay.

1.4 Fair Play — Zero Tolerance

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Cheating
  • Hacking
  • Exploiting bugs
  • Macros
  • Unauthorized third-party software
  • Scripting or automation
  • Ghosting
  • Stream sniping
  • Receiving outside assistance
  • Intentional teaming with opponents

Confirmed cheating or unauthorized software can result in immediate disqualification with no warning tier.

1.5 Player Conduct

All players, coaches and spectators must maintain professional behaviour.

The following are prohibited:

  • Toxicity
  • Harassment
  • Discrimination
  • Offensive or abusive language
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct
  • Disruptive behaviour around competition areas

Players must follow instructions from Tournament Officials at all times.

1.6 Technical Issues

  • Officials may grant technical pauses for legitimate hardware, software or connectivity issues.
  • Officials decide whether a match should continue, pause or restart.
  • Repeated technical problems caused by a player's own equipment may result in forfeiture.
  • A replay is not automatically granted because of a player-side technical problem.

1.7 Disputes

  • Only the team captain may raise a dispute for team tournaments.
  • For individual tournaments, the individual player may raise the dispute.
  • Disputes must be raised within 10 minutes of the match ending.
  • Screenshots, recordings or match logs should be provided where possible.
  • Tournament Officials will review the evidence and issue a ruling.
  • Tournament Officials' decisions are final and binding.

1.8 Penalties

Depending on the severity and frequency of an offence, officials may issue:

  1. Formal Warning
  2. Match Forfeit
  3. Player/Team Disqualification

Repeated no-shows may result in removal from the remainder of the tournament.

2. VALORANT

2.1 Tournament Structure

  • 16 teams
  • 5 main players + 1 substitute
  • Single Elimination
  • Best of 1 (BO1) in every round
  • PC tournament
  • Latest live Valorant patch
  • Entry fee: ₹750 per team

The bracket consists of:

  • Round of 16 — 8 matches
  • Quarterfinals — 4 matches
  • Semifinals — 2 matches
  • Grand Final — 1 match

An optional third-place match may be held between the semifinal losers if time permits.

2.2 Roster

  • 5 main players are required.
  • 1 substitute may be registered.
  • No roster changes after registration closes.
  • Only registered players may participate.

2.3 Seeding

The standard 16-team bracket pairing is:

  • 1 vs 16
  • 8 vs 9
  • 5 vs 12
  • 4 vs 13
  • 3 vs 14
  • 6 vs 11
  • 7 vs 10
  • 2 vs 15

2.4 Map Selection

For BO1:

  1. Knife round or coin toss determines which team bans first.
  2. Teams alternate banning maps.
  3. The remaining map is played.
  4. Server selection is determined by Tournament Officials.

2.5 Game Settings

  • Latest live patch
  • Standard Tournament/Competitive settings
  • All agents are allowed unless disabled by Riot Games
  • Tournament Officials determine server selection

2.6 Match Rules

  • Teams must be ready 15 minutes before their match.
  • Failure to join within 10 minutes may result in a forfeit.
  • Organizers create and manage match lobbies.
  • Cheating, scripting, exploiting and unauthorized software are prohibited.
  • Stream sniping and ghosting are prohibited.
  • Outside assistance is prohibited.

3. BGMI

  • 25 squads
  • 4 players per squad + 1 optional substitute
  • Squad TPP
  • Single continuous lobby
  • 10 total matches
  • 5 matches per day
  • No knockout or qualifier stage
  • Champion is determined by cumulative points after Match 10.

All 25 squads play all 10 matches.

3.4 Kill Points

  • 1 point per elimination
  • Match Score = Placement Points + Kill Points

3.5 BGMI Tie-Breakers

If squads finish with equal total points:

  1. Higher placement points
  2. Greater number of Chicken Dinners/WWCD
  3. Higher total kill points
  4. Better placement in Match 10

3.6 BGMI Specific Rules

  • Players must use their registered in-game IDs.
  • No emulator.
  • No unauthorized third-party software.
  • Cheating, teaming, exploitation or unfair play results in immediate disqualification.
  • Squads must report at least 20 minutes before their first match.

5. EA SPORTS FC 26

5.1 Tournament Structure

  • 32 individual players
  • 1v1
  • Single Elimination
  • Entry fee: ₹250 per player
  • PC
  • Projector screen provided
  • Players should bring their own controller.

Bracket:

  • Round of 32 — 16 matches
  • Round of 16 — 8 matches
  • Quarterfinals — 4 matches
  • Semifinals — 2 matches
  • Third-Place Match — 1 match
  • Grand Final — 1 match

5.2 Match Settings

  • Half length: 3 minutes
  • Difficulty: Legendary
  • Competitive Mode: ON
  • Extra Time: ON for knockout matches
  • Penalties: ON for knockout matches
  • Latest Official Squads
  • Custom Squads: OFF
  • Platform: PC

These settings are intended to keep matches skill-based and prevent squad-building advantages.

5.3 Player Rules

  • Each player can participate only once.
  • Players must report 15 minutes before their match.
  • Failure to appear within 10 minutes may result in forfeiture.
  • Match results must be reported immediately after the match.
  • Players are responsible for their controllers.

6. TEKKEN 8

6.1 Tournament Structure

  • 32 individual players
  • 1v1
  • Single Elimination
  • Entry fee: ₹200 per player
  • Projector screen provided
  • Bring your own controller.

Unlike team tournaments, Tekken 8 has:

  • No team roster
  • No substitute
  • No captain
  • Individual registration only

6.2 Match Format

  • Round of 32 — 16 matches
  • Round of 16 — 8 matches
  • Quarterfinals — 4 matches
  • Semifinals — 2 matches
  • Grand Final — BO5

All rounds before the Grand Final are Best of 3.

The Grand Final is Best of 5.

6.3 Game Settings

  • Latest live Tekken 8 patch
  • Standard Tournament/Competitive settings
  • Coin toss determines the stage-selection procedure.

6.4 Match Rules

  • Players must report at least 15 minutes before their match.
  • Failure to join within 10 minutes may result in forfeiture.
  • Match results must be reported immediately.
  • Cheating, hacking, scripting and exploiting bugs are prohibited.
  • Outside assistance is prohibited.
  • Violations may result in immediate disqualification.

7. COMMON ZERO-TOLERANCE POLICY

The following can result in immediate disqualification across all five games:

  • Hacking
  • Cheating
  • Exploiting bugs
  • Unauthorized software
  • Macros
  • Scripting
  • Ghosting
  • Stream sniping
  • Outside assistance
  • Intentional teaming
  • Serious misconduct

Harassment, discrimination, toxicity and offensive behaviour are also prohibited.

8. FINAL AUTHORITY

Tournament Officials have the authority to:

  • Investigate suspected violations
  • Request evidence
  • Pause or restart matches when technically justified
  • Issue warnings or forfeits
  • Disqualify players or teams
  • Adjust schedules where necessary
  • Resolve situations not explicitly covered in the handbook

All decisions of the Organizing Committee and Tournament Officials are final and binding.
