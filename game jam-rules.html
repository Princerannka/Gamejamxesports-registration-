GAME JAM

OFFICIAL RULES

Please read carefully before participating

GENERAL CONDUCT

  • No indecency allowed
  • No fighting the staff
  • No public displays of affection (PDA)
  • No littering
  • No making a scene or causing disturbances
  • No cheating or any form of sabotage allowed
  • Judge decision is final

CONSEQUENCE: Violations of general conduct rules range from a light time penalty to immediate disqualification.

TECHNICAL RULES

  • No AI is allowed in the game itself
  • AI is only permitted for coding assistance
  • Any and all assets may be created, downloaded, or pre-made and brought to the event
    • Taking assets from fellow participants is not allowed
  • Game size is limited to 2GB
  • Templates from the engine used are allowed
    • Using pre-made games and editing them is not allowed
  • Any plagiarism found will result in immediate disqualification

GAME RULES

  • The submitted game must not include sexual or inappropriate content
  • ALL games must be made during the event
  • A 30-second gameplay clip must be submitted during the creation of the game
  • Don't push one aspect of the game to an extreme

By participating in this game jam, you agree to abide by all rules listed above.
