6. TEKKEN 8

6.1 Tournament Structure

  • 32 individual players
  • 1v1
  • Single Elimination
  • Entry fee: ₹200 per player
  • Projector screen provided
  • Bring your own controller.

Unlike team tournaments, Tekken 8 has:

  • No team roster
  • No substitute
  • No captain
  • Individual registration only

6.2 Match Format

  • Round of 32 — 16 matches
  • Round of 16 — 8 matches
  • Quarterfinals — 4 matches
  • Semifinals — 2 matches
  • Grand Final — BO5

All rounds before the Grand Final are Best of 3.

The Grand Final is Best of 5.

6.3 Game Settings

  • Latest live Tekken 8 patch
  • Standard Tournament/Competitive settings
  • Coin toss determines the stage-selection procedure.

6.4 Match Rules

  • Players must report at least 15 minutes before their match.
  • Failure to join within 10 minutes may result in forfeiture.
  • Match results must be reported immediately.
  • Cheating, hacking, scripting and exploiting bugs are prohibited.
  • Outside assistance is prohibited.
  • Violations may result in immediate disqualification.
