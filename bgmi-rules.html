3. BGMI

3.1 Tournament Structure

  • 25 squads
  • 4 players per squad + 1 optional substitute
  • Squad TPP
  • Single continuous lobby
  • 10 total matches
  • 5 matches per day
  • No knockout or qualifier stage
  • Champion is determined by cumulative points after Match 10.

All 25 squads play all 10 matches.

3.4 Kill Points

  • 1 point per elimination
  • Match Score = Placement Points + Kill Points
  • Scores accumulate across all 10 matches.

3.5 BGMI Tie-Breakers

If squads finish with equal total points:

  1. Higher placement points
  2. Greater number of Chicken Dinners/WWCD
  3. Higher total kill points
  4. Better placement in Match 10

3.6 BGMI Specific Rules

  • Players must use their registered in-game IDs.
  • No emulator.
  • No unauthorized third-party software.
  • Cheating, teaming, exploitation or unfair play results in immediate disqualification.
  • Squads must report at least 20 minutes before their first match.
